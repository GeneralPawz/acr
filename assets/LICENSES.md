Notes:

- Animated Free Bison: Sketchfab download (FBX + textures), kept outside the repo
  (`ACR_SOURCES/sketchfab/animated-free-bison`); 61k triangles decimated to 12k, scaled to
  1.85 m at the hump, base colour only. Its material and image names (`tripo_*`) suggest the
  mesh was generated with Tripo AI. Credit line: "Animated Free Bison" by AneesAnimates,
  CC BY 4.0, modified (decimated, rescaled, clips added).

- WildMesh Standard License v1.1 (read 2026-10-22), in short: for licensees with at most
  USD 100,000 revenue or funding in 12 months (above that a Studio License is required);
  non-exclusive, perpetual, commercial use allowed; may be modified, retextured,
  re-animated, optimised (s. 3d); end products may ship them only in compiled, packaged or
  otherwise non-extractable form (s. 3b). Not allowed: redistributing the assets or
  substantial derivatives as files (originals or modified), putting them in open
  repositories or download links, training AI/ML on them, claiming authorship (s. 4).
  Attribution not required; suggested credit: "3D Animals by WildMesh 3D —
  https://www.patreon.com/wildmesh". Consequences for ACR: the pack and the `.glb` files
  built from it stay out of git and out of any public download (a game build that ships
  loose `.glb` files would count as extractable: needs packing or a decision before any
  release); the repository holds only the build scripts.

- Horse Rig: downloaded 2026-10-22 from the Objaverse mirror of Sketchfab's public
  downloads (`huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse`, `glbs/000-125/<uid>.glb`), the
  Sketchfab glTF conversion: 38.8k + 3.2k triangles, 46-joint skin, three PNG textures.
  The licence is stated on the Sketchfab page (API: `license.slug = by`) and in the file's
  `asset.extras` (author, licence, source). Credit line: "Horse Rig" by CreativeMachine,
  CC BY 4.0, modified (decimated, refitted to rig R, repainted).
- Ground textures (Poly Haven, CC0, no attribution required; credited anyway): the 2k JPG
  maps (diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness, displacement) fetched by
  `assets/ground/fetch_polyhaven.py` into `D:\ACR-models\polyhaven-textures\<id>\` (with
  `SOURCE.json`), packed to 1024² PNGs by `cargo xtask ground` (`assets/ground/pack.py`) into
  `<out>/ground/`. Not committed.
- Ships (Poly Haven, CC0, no attribution required; credited anyway): the 2k glTF downloads,
  kept outside the repo under `$ACR_SOURCES/polyhaven/<id>/` (base colour textures only).
  Authors per `SOURCE.json`: James Ray Cock (model, textures, clean-up), Rico Cilliers
  (sails), Nicolò Zubbini (original model). Decimated, re-oriented, scaled, cut at the
  waterline, rigged and animated (`assets/blender/lib/acr_assets/ships.py`).
- 18th Century Cannon: downloaded 2026-10-22 from Sketchfab (`18th-century-cannon.zip`,
  FBX + PBR PNGs), kept under `$ACR_SOURCES/sketchfab/18th-century-cannon/`. Only the barrel
  and its albedo texture are used; the field carriage and wheels are ours. Credit line:
  "18th Century Cannon" by rivetech, CC BY 4.0, modified (barrel only, on a new carriage,
  animated).

## MakeHuman 1.x (CC0 1.0)

- **Files:** `assets/blender/data/makehuman/head.json` (#283) and
  `assets/blender/data/makehuman/body.json` (#287).
- **Source:** <https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman>, commit
  `a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482`:
  - the base mesh `makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj` (groups `body`, `helper-l-eye`,
    `helper-r-eye`): the head and upper neck, with the eyeballs;
  - the targets under `makehuman/data/targets/` (`macrodetails/caucasian-male-*`,
    `macrodetails/universal-male-*-averagemuscle-*`, `chin`, `cheek`, `nose`, `eyebrows`,
    `forehead`, `eyes`, `head`). The file lists the targets behind each `face_*` morph.
- **Licence:** CC0 1.0 Universal. The repository's `LICENSE.md`, section C, says "The base
  mesh and proxies, Targets and modifiers, Textures, Clothes, Poses and expressions ...
  have been released under CC0 1.0 Universal". The full text is in `LICENSE.ASSETS.md`, and
  each file's header says "This asset was explicitly released as CC0 in september 2020".
  The copyright holders at release were Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas
  Hauquier.
- **How it was derived:**
  - The base mesh was set to a young adult man (macro targets
    `caucasian-male-young` and `universal-male-young-averagemuscle-averageweight`, both at
    weight 1).
  - It was cut about 4 cm below the chin and converted to metres with z up and -y
    forward, in 0.1 mm integers.
  - The listed targets were summed into the ten `face_*` morphs and limited to the
    head's vertices.
  - Made by `assets/blender/tools/makehuman_head.py`. The build decimates the head (`acr_assets/mhead.py`).
  - No MakeHuman code is used. The build does not need the MakeHuman download.
  - `body.json`: the same young adult man's `body` group below the chin (no head, no
    helpers) and the centres of the base mesh's `joint-*` helper cubes, in the same units.
    Made by `assets/blender/tools/makehuman_body.py`; the build reposes it onto skeleton H
    and decimates it (`acr_assets/mbody.py`).
