Hit, pain, agony and panic sounds of ACR (issues #243, #248). The original game has none; these are CC0 (public
domain dedication, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and installed as
ACR\Sounds\ in the game folder (docs/simulation.md, "Hit and pain sounds").

hit_body1-3.ogg   Kenney, "Impact Sounds" 1.0 (www.kenney.nl), CC0:
                  impactPunch_medium_000-002.ogg, unchanged
hit_heavy1-3.ogg  Kenney, "Impact Sounds": impactPunch_heavy_000-002.ogg, unchanged
hit_wood1-3.ogg   Kenney, "Impact Sounds": impactWood_heavy_000-002.ogg, unchanged

pain1-9.wav       HaelDB, "Male Grunt/Yelling sounds"
                  (https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds), used under its
                  CC0 option (offered as CC0 or OGA-BY 3.0):
                  3grunt1-5.wav, 1yell1.wav, 1yell8.wav, 2yell1.wav, 3yell3.wav,
                  converted to mono 22050 Hz and trimmed of silence.

scream1-6.wav     HaelDB, "Male Grunt/Yelling sounds" (as above, CC0 option), agony screams:
                  yell1.wav, 3yell13.wav, 3yell16.wav, 1yell3.wav, 2yell6.wav, 3yell6.wav,
                  mono 22050 Hz 8-bit, trimmed of silence, cut to 3 s, faded out.
moan1-5.wav       the same pack: 3grunt1.wav, 3grunt2.wav, 3grunt6.wav, 3grunt3.wav,
                  1yell13.wav, slowed to 0.65-0.8 (lower), mono 22050 Hz 8-bit, faded out.
gasp1-3.wav       the same pack: 3grunt4.wav, 3grunt5.wav, 3grunt3.wav, slowed to 0.9,
                  mono 22050 Hz 8-bit.
fear1-7.wav       the same pack: 1yell11.wav, 2yell4.wav, 2yell5.wav, 3yell14.wav,
                  yell10.wav, yell2.wav, yell5.wav, mono 22050 Hz 8-bit, cut to 1.8 s,
                  faded out.
