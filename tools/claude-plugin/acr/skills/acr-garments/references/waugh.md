Check an offset before relying on it: render the page's top 120 pt and read the page number.

**Scale:** the cutting diagrams carry an inch ruler in the left margin (ticks every inch, labelled 1–10, 20, 30, …), drawn to the same scale as the panels; Diagram XIII has a 0–50 cm bar instead. The tailors' diagrams (book pp. 39–42, 93–98) are reproductions of period cutting layouts with no reliable scale: use them for the cut's shape and seam placement, not for dimensions. Waugh's own diagrams are taken from surviving garments and show the cut pieces as made; read the caption for what is turned back (cuffs cut in one with the sleeve, flounces) and treat the outline as the seam line unless the caption says otherwise.
